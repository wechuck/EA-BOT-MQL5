## Key Success Factors

### **1. Signal Quality Over Quantity**
- Maximum 1 signal per day
- Target 4-7 per week
- NEVER compromise on setup quality

### **2. Strict Risk Management**
- Never exceed planned risk percentage
- Use aggressive mode sparingly (10-20% of trades maximum)
- Always set SL/TP automatically after entry

### **3. Execution Protection**
- Monitor spread constantly
- Avoid trading during high spread
- Protect against slippage

### **4. Trade Management**
- Let EA handle SL/TP placement
- Trust the trailing stop system
- Don't interfere with trade management

### **5. Psychological Discipline**
- Accept losses as part of the process
- Don't chase missed opportunities
- Stick to the plan during drawdowns
